# Code of Conduct

We want contributors and users to participate in a welcoming, constructive
community. Be respectful, assume good faith, give actionable feedback, and
avoid harassment, discrimination, threats and disclosure of private information.

Maintainers may moderate discussions, remove abusive content and restrict
participation to keep collaboration safe. To report misconduct, use GitHub's
private contact or reporting features where available. For conduct reports,
do not disclose personal information in public issues.
